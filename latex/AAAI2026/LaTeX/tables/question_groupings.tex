Full operation and entity labels are shown here; some compact appendix plots merge low-support operation labels (\textit{comparative}, \textit{causal}, \textit{temporal}, \textit{text reading}, \textit{world knowledge}) into \textit{other} for readability.}

\toprule
Axis & Group & Definition / typical cue \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Reasoning operation}} \\
& Attribute & asks for color, material, type, name, state, or other descriptive property \\
& Counting & asks how many or how much \\
& Identity & asks what/who/which entity is present or being referred to \\
& Action & asks what someone or something is doing \\
& Spatial relation / location & asks where something is or about explicit relations such as left/right, behind, on, in, or between \\
& Existence & asks whether something is present at all (e.g., ``is there'', ``are there any'') \\
& Comparative & asks same/different, more/less, bigger/smaller, higher/lower, or other comparison \\
& Causal & asks why something is happening \\
& Temporal & asks about age, duration, before/after, time, or sequence \\
& Text reading & requires reading visible text, numbers, signs, labels, or logos \\
& World knowledge & requires extra-visual commonsense or factual knowledge beyond simple visual attributes \\
& Other & residual cases that do not fit the categories above \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{l}{\textit{Entity type}} \\
& Person & humans or person roles \\
& Animal & animals or animal kinds \\
& Object & generic physical objects not better captured by the more specific entity groups \\
& Vehicle & cars, buses, bikes, planes, boats, and related vehicles \\
& Food & food, drink, ingredients, or dishes \\
& Product & named or branded consumer products and artifacts \\
& Place & locations, buildings, rooms, or geographic scenes \\
& Text & text-bearing entities when the visible writing itself is central \\
& Other & abstract, mixed, or uncategorizable referents (e.g., sport, weather, information) \\
